How it works

  1. 01

    Connect

    Type a GitHub username or drop a resume. Only public data is read, and resumes are parsed on your device.

  2. 02

    Review

    Check exactly what goes into the portfolio. Uncheck anything you would rather leave out.

  3. 03

    Keep

    Tune the theme and typography, then download a standalone HTML file or save it and share a link.

Eight themes

Each theme is designed as its own world: palette, surfaces, link treatment and button voice. Pick one in the Customize panel after you generate.

Four type systems

Typography does the talking. Four complete voices, from a confident grotesk to full monospace.
