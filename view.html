From GitHub

Your code becomes the portfolio.

Repositories ranked by stars, README excerpts pulled automatically, language mix charted, activity drawn as a timeline, and social links wired up. Every forge reads the latest public data.

From a resume

No GitHub? No problem.

Drop a PDF, DOCX or text resume. It is parsed on your device, you review what goes in, and experience, projects and skills become proper sections.
